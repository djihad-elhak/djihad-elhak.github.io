01

How it works

A workshop appointment system: customers book a service for their car, staff on a brand team move each car through arrival and repair, and an administrator manages brands, services, teams and blocked dates in a Django admin themed with Jazzmin. I built the backend API.

  • Accounts: customers and staff are separate user models, each with its own JWT login; customers can reset their password with a six-digit emailed token.
  • Status emails: HTML templates for arrived, did not arrive, repaired and irreparable.
  • Capacity: a signal keeps a per-day booking count; full or blocked days are returned to the calendar.
  • Data model: brands, services per brand, teams per brand, bookings with many services, a history snapshot and a contact form.
