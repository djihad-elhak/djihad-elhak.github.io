# djihad-elhak.github.io

Source of my portfolio: **https://djihad-elhak.github.io**

Static pages, no build step at serve time and no tracking: `index.html`, one case study per project in `projects/`, shared `style.css`. Screenshots are in `img/`, the Andrology Console video in `media/`. Client identifiers in the AutoCare screenshots are blurred; the patients and operators shown in the Andrology Console are test data. The typefaces, Newsreader and IBM Plex, are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License (texts in `fonts/`). Organisation logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify where I studied and worked.
