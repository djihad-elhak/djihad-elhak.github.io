# djihad-elhak.github.io

Source of my portfolio: **https://djihad-elhak.github.io**

Static pages with no build step at serve time and no tracking:
- `index.html`: the home page.
- `cv.html`: the CV. `cv.pdf` is printed from it.
- `projects/`: one case study per project.
- `style.css` and `site.js`: shared by every page.

The design rationale, with the reference measurements and every token, is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

Screenshots are in `img/`, and the project images on the home page are in `img/media/`. The Andrology Console video is in `media/`.

Client identifiers in the AutoCare screenshots are blurred. The patients and operators shown in the Andrology Console are test data.

The typeface, Inter, is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License (`fonts/OFL-inter.txt`). Organisation logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify where I studied and worked.
