Computer vision and applied AI

I build computer vision and AI systems and take them all the way to the people who use them. At CERIST, I replaced a YOLO11m and DeepSORT pipeline with a 0.38M‑parameter detector and a motion-only tracker that analyse a sperm motility clip in 4.6 seconds on two CPU cores, and shipped them in a desktop application for a reproductive biology laboratory.

I also build LLM systems that run on a team's own GPUs: retrieval over code, agents that write and check their own tests, and the evaluation around them.

I am in the M2 Data, Knowledge and Hybrid AI at Université Paris-Saclay and looking for a 4 to 6 month end‑of‑studies internship from February 2027, in computer vision, AI for health or applied machine learning. Email me or read my CV.
